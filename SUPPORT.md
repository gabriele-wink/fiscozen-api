# Help & support

**fiscozen-api** is an independent, unofficial project: it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Fiscozen S.p.A.

- **Help and questions:** [gabriele@windroses.world](mailto:gabriele@windroses.world)
- **Bugs and feature requests:** [open an issue](https://github.com/gabriele-wink/fiscozen-api/issues)
- **Problems with your Fiscozen account** (login, invoices, SDI rejections, billing): contact Fiscozen directly, not this project.

Never send your password, your SMS code or your `.session.json` file, by email or in an issue.
